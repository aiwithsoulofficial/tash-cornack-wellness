The workload, first

This is the part nobody puts in the sales pitch, and it's the part that decides whether this is right for you this year.

5 to 10 hours a week, minimum

Even at the low end that's 45 to 60 minutes a day, seven days a week, just on basic operations - posting, replying to members, moderating. That's before you make any courses.

And that's for one community, run without automation.

What they charge, and how many members

Median price across paid communities is $27 a month. Most that work land between $29 and $99. Cheaper groups play a volume game.

The one number that decides it

The difference between a community that stalls at $5K and one that doesn't isn't the price. It's whether you run paid group programs on top of the subscription. Content behind a paywall plateaus. Content plus live cohorts roughly doubles the annual figure.

Which means more delivery, not less.
