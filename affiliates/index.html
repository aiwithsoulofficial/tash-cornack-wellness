Two you should skip, and why

Melaleuca is not an affiliate program

It's a membership. $25 plus GST to join, and you commit to ordering 35 Product Points every single month to stay eligible. Commissions only come from people you personally enrol into your organisation.

That means paying monthly for the right to earn, and running a second downline alongside the water. Published figures put Directors - the first real business-builder rank - at an average of around $2,000 a year.

If you love the products, stay a customer and take the member discount. Just don't count it as an income stream, and don't build it into the ladder.

Amazon isn't worth the effort

Health and personal care pays roughly 1%. That's about 40 cents on a $40 supplement. Your time is better spent on any single program above.
